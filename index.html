Questions

How many Genesis NFTs are there?

5,000 in total. Whitelisted wallets mint first at $10. Anything left after that opens to the public.

How does the 30% profit share work?

Every month we calculate Chart Funded's profit, subtract expenses, and set aside 30% of the remaining net profit for Genesis holders. Net profit can be zero in some months, so payouts aren't guaranteed. Full terms are published before mint.

What is the Trade Algo?

Our automated trading algo with 4 strategies. It normally costs $299 a month. Genesis holders get it free for the first 6 months.

Does holding the NFT get me a funded account?

It gets you priority and no fee. Your application is reviewed first, but you still need to meet the funding rules.

What's the difference between Step 1 and Instant Funding?

Step 1 ($69) is an evaluation you trade under our rules before getting funded. Instant Funding ($89) skips the evaluation and gives real capital to traders who show a live MT5 account with at least 3 months of history and pass our questions.

Is joining the whitelist free?

Yes. The whitelist costs nothing. You only pay the $10 mint price if you mint.

Do I need to connect my wallet?

No. You only paste your address. This site never asks you to connect or sign anything. If a site does, it isn't us.

Which wallet should I use?

Your own EVM wallet, like MetaMask, Trust Wallet or Rabby. Don't use an exchange deposit address, because you won't be able to hold or see the NFT there.

How are the whitelist tasks checked?

The team reviews every entry before mint. If you unfollow on X or leave Telegram or Discord, your spot can be removed.

Stay safe

  • We never ask for your seed phrase or private key.
